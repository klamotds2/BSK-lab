=== BSK Task 2 - External connectivity and firewall tests ===

Test host:
bsk-client -> bsk-server (172.17.0.2)

--- Before UFW ---
22/tcp   SSH        reachable
80/tcp   HTTP       reachable
443/tcp  HTTPS      reachable
9000/tcp test       reachable

--- After UFW ---

22/tcp - SSH:
Connection to 172.17.0.2 port 22 succeeded

80/tcp - HTTP:
Connection to 172.17.0.2 port 80 succeeded

443/tcp - HTTPS:
Connection to 172.17.0.2 port 443 succeeded

9000/tcp - unauthorized test service:
Connection timed out.

Important:
Port 9000 remained in LISTEN state on bsk-server during the test.
Therefore the timeout demonstrates firewall filtering rather than the
absence of a listening service.

--- Application tests after enabling UFW ---

HTTP:
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Server: Apache/2.4.58 (Ubuntu)

HTTPS:
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Server: Apache/2.4.58 (Ubuntu)

Result:
The firewall permits the required SSH, HTTP and HTTPS services while
blocking other unsolicited incoming connections by default.
