=== BSK Task 2 - Password policy for a small company ===

1. Passwords should contain at least 14 characters. Longer passphrases are
   preferred over short passwords with predictable substitutions.

2. Common passwords such as "admin", "student", "password" and "qwerty123"
   must not be permitted.

3. Passwords based on predictable company, university, city, department,
   product or year combinations should be avoided.

4. Complexity alone must not be considered sufficient protection. The
   laboratory password "Linux!Lab2026" contains different character classes,
   but was still recovered because it appeared in the supplied dictionary.

5. Every service and account should use a unique password. Password reuse
   increases the impact of credential disclosure.

6. Multi-factor authentication should be enabled wherever supported,
   particularly for administrative accounts and remote access.

7. Users should use an approved password manager to generate and store unique,
   high-entropy passwords.

8. Passwords should be changed immediately when compromise is suspected.
   Routine forced changes without evidence of compromise should not encourage
   predictable password modifications such as incrementing a year or number.

Conclusion:
The laboratory dictionary attack recovered 6 of 8 hashes (75%). This shows
that predictable passwords remain vulnerable even when some include uppercase
characters, digits and symbols. Password length, uniqueness and resistance to
dictionary guessing are therefore more important than superficial complexity.
