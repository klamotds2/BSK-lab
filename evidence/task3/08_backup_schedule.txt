=== BSK Task 3 - Backup schedule ===

--- Schedule ---
SHELL=/bin/bash
PATH=/usr/local/sbin:/usr/local/bin:/usr/sbin:/usr/bin:/sbin:/bin

0 2 * * * root /usr/local/sbin/bsk-backup.sh

--- Backup script ---
#!/bin/bash
/usr/bin/rsync -a --delete /firma/ /backup/firma/ >> /var/log/bsk-backup.log 2>&1

--- Permissions ---
-rw-r--r-- 1 root root 128 Nov  7 13:02 /etc/cron.d/bsk-backup
-rwxr-x--- 1 root root  94 Nov  7 13:02 /usr/local/sbin/bsk-backup.sh

--- Cron process ---
root        1132  0.0  0.0   3820  1916 ?        Ss   13:03   0:00 cron

--- Justification ---
The backup is scheduled daily at 02:00.
This time was selected as a typical low-activity period.
Daily execution limits the potential loss window to approximately 24 hours.
rsync transfers only changed data after the initial copy, reducing repeated I/O.
The backup command is stored in a root-owned script rather than duplicated in cron.
